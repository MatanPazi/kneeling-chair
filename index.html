Calculation

Height from the user's dimensions:

H = F × sin(22.5°) + T × sin(22.5°) + FO × cos(22.5°)

With a 5 cm safety margin:

H_beam = H − 5

Height provided by the beam:

H_beam = L × cos(22.5°) + CS + W

Therefore:

L = (H − 5 − CS − W) / cos(22.5°)
